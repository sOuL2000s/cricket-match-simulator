Bulk Edit Team Roster

Enter your team and player names with roles below. The AI will parse it.
Format:

[Team A Name]:
Player Name 1 (Batter)
Player Name 2 (Bowler)
... (up to 11 players)

[Team B Name]:
Player Name X (All Rounder)
... (up to 11 players)

Valid roles: Batter, Wicket Keeper, All Rounder, Bowler. If a role is missing or unclear, it defaults to 'Batter'.
